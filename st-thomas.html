St. Thomas Tours

The most-visited of the U.S. Virgin Islands — duty-free shopping in Charlotte Amalie, charter boats out of Red Hook.

About St. Thomas

St. Thomas is the largest and busiest of the three U.S. Virgin Islands. Charlotte Amalie, on the south coast, is one of the Caribbean's largest cruise ports — up to four ships in a day during peak winter weeks. Most charter boats and snorkel tours depart from either the Charlotte Amalie waterfront or Red Hook (on the east end), which is closer to the protected snorkeling at Coki Beach and a 10-minute ferry from St. John.

If you're staying on St. Thomas and want to get on the water, the typical day is a half-day catamaran charter that hits Buck Island, Christmas Cove, or Honeymoon Beach (on the small Water Island). Full-day tours often add a stop at Trunk Bay on St. John. Operators we work with include independent captain-owner outfits and a few of the larger catamaran companies. Cell coverage is good across the island except in the deep coves; AT&T and T-Mobile both work without an international plan since the USVI is U.S. soil.

What to know before you book

  • Cruise ship traffic peaks Dec-April; book private charters 2+ weeks ahead during cruise weeks
  • Red Hook (east end) is the launching point for most St. John ferries and east-side snorkel tours
  • Charlotte Amalie has the largest selection of catamaran day-trip operators
  • No passport needed — USVI is U.S. soil; domestic flights apply
  • Hurricane season is June-November; most operators offer 24-hour reschedule for weather
  • Cell service: U.S. carriers work without roaming charges
