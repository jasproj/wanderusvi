Types of Fishing

🎣 Inshore / Light Tackle

Half-day shallow-water fishing. Snapper, jacks, and reef species. Family-friendly.

🐟 Offshore / Deep Sea

Full-day blue-water trolling for marlin, mahi, tuna, wahoo. Bigger boats, longer runs.

🏞️ Freshwater / River

Trout, salmon, pike, or coarse fishing depending on region. Wading or boat-based.

Best Fishing Spots

North Drop (off St. Thomas)

USVI offshore

Top-tier blue marlin grounds. The North Drop is 25 miles offshore where the seafloor falls from 100ft to 1000ft+.

Lang Bank (St. Croix)

USVI

Tuna, mahi-mahi, wahoo. Day-charter friendly distance from Christiansted.

Buck Island Channel (St. Croix)

St. Croix

Light-tackle fishing for snapper, grouper, jacks. Half-day trips from Christiansted.

Mid-channel (St. Thomas to BVI)

USVI/BVI

Sailfish, dorado, kingfish. Most charters run mid-channel drifts.

South Side Drop (St. John)

USVI

Wahoo run November-March. Charter from Cruz Bay; calmer than St. Thomas runs.

Salt River drop-off (St. Croix)

St. Croix

Reef fishing for grouper and yellowtail. Closer-in option for half-day trips.

Fishing Tips

🌅 Dawn departures

Most charters leave before sunrise. Plan your sleep accordingly.

🤢 Sea-sickness meds

Even half-day inshore can rough up — take meds the night before.

💵 Tip the mate

Crew is partly tip-funded. 15-20% of charter cost is standard.

🐟 Catch policy

Confirm catch-and-release vs. take-home before booking. Some species are protected.
