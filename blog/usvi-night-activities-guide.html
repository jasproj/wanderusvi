St. Croix Has a Bioluminescent Bay — Plus What Else to Do in the USVI at Night

Updated October 25, 2026 • 4 min read

Quick correction to something we used to say here: the US Virgin Islands do have a bioluminescent bay. It is Salt River Bay on the north shore of St. Croix, inside the Salt River Bay National Historical Park and Ecological Preserve. The glow comes from the same dinoflagellates that light up Puerto Rico's bays; it is dimmer than Mosquito Bay on Vieques, but on a dark night with a clear kayak under you it is plenty.

Salt River Bay Bioluminescent Kayak Tours (St. Croix)

Two operators run night paddles from the Salt River marina area, both about two hours on the water:

  • Sea Thru Kayaks VI — the only fully see-through kayak tour on the bay, so the glow shows under your feet as well as off your paddle. From $59.47 per adult. Check availability →
  • BushTribe Eco Adventures — bioluminescent kayak trip in an ocean kayak ($50 per person) or clear kayak ($60), two per boat. Check availability →

Book around the new moon. Tours run on moonless or early-moonset nights because moonlight washes the glow out; operators publish the dates they run. Bring a dry bag, skip the bug spray until after you are off the water (DEET harms the organisms), and expect to get wet.

St. Thomas and St. John do not have a bio bay, but they have their own after-dark trips. Here is what is worth doing when the sun goes down.

Night Snorkeling & Diving

Several operators on St. Thomas and St. Croix offer night dives and snorkel trips. You'll see octopus, lobster, and reef fish that hide during the day. Buck Island off St. Croix is particularly good for this.

Sunset/Night Snorkel — Aqua Marine Dive Center, St. Thomas

Sunset Sails

This is where USVI shines. Dozens of catamaran and sailboat operators run sunset cruises—most include drinks and some include dinner. The views across the harbor as the sun drops behind the islands are legitimately spectacular.

Sunset Sail with Cocktails and Appetizers — The VI Cat, St. Thomas

$85 per adult ($40 child) · priced per person

Check Availability

Stargazing

Less light pollution than Puerto Rico or Hawaii. St. John in particular has dark skies—Virgin Islands National Park covers most of the island. No formal stargazing tours that we know of, but the beaches at night are worth it.

Night Kayaking

A few operators offer LED-lit kayak tours in calm bays. Not bioluminescence—just a fun way to see the water at night with underwater lights on your kayak.

If You Want a Brighter Bio Bay

Puerto Rico's three bays glow harder than Salt River, Mosquito Bay on Vieques most of all. Fly to Puerto Rico. It's a short flight from St. Thomas to San Juan, and from there you can reach Fajardo (1 hour drive) or Vieques (ferry or puddle jumper). We have a separate site for that: wanderpuertorico.com/bio-bay

Some people combine USVI + PR in one trip. Different vibe—USVI is quieter and more beach-focused, PR has more nightlife and activities. Both worth doing.
